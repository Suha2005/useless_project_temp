«Because you don't need soft validation. You need to be roasted to rock bottom.»

What Is RAGEBAITER?

Most AI assistants will politely coddle you when you ask if you should text your ex at 3 AM or explain why you skipped class because your bed was too warm.

RAGEBAITER does the opposite. It takes whatever dilemma, terrible financial choice, delusional career plan, or lazy excuse you submit, and roasts you into the earth's mantle while serving the most infuriating, condescending, toxic advice imaginable.

Objective: Make you laugh, question your life choices, and close the app in a fit of comedic rage.

Tech Stack

  • 🔥 Backend: Python 3.9+, Flask, SQLAlchemy, Werkzeug
  • 🧠 AI & Heuristics: Dual-engine AI (Gemini / OpenAI) with an autonomous deterministic local roast engine
  • ⚡ Frontend: Clean Vanilla CSS, modern responsive dark mode, Web Audio API sound effects
  • 💾 Database: SQLite with User history and public Wall of Shame
⚠ DISCLAIMER: RAGEBAITER is purely satirical entertainment. None of the unhelpful advice provided should ever be followed in the real world. If you text your ex because RAGEBAITER told you to, that is entirely between you and your therapist.
